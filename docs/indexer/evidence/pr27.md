[method.md](../method.md). Live outputs name the install's tenants, so they stay outside the repository and
snapshots are given by number.

## 6. What the judgment found afterwards (2026-10-26)

A new judgment on the corpus found one wrong edge the PR added: elsa's `x.GetInput<string>(item)` reached a method
that is not generic. The cause is older than the PR: a call written with type arguments was never checked against the
method's type parameters. It reaches 104 edges in the 15 C# repos of the protocol: 24 to an extension block's method
(elsa, smartstore), 80 to an extension with a `this` parameter (elsa 45, radarr 18, umbraco 9, smartstore 6, jellyfin
1, notifo 1). The fix is a PR of its own.

## 7. What went wrong in the process

- The session that ran the validation stopped before the after side. It was done two days later.
- The install's Neo4j looped on restart until the author recreated the container, so the after side waited.
- `aidd-indexer:local` moved from image `f07550cbe2e2` to `9e6dd3753826` with no event Docker kept. `micro.sh` keys its
  base by the image id, so a new base is built.
- The "before" picture cannot be retaken once the reinstall is done.
